## Abstract

This paper argues that the strategic inflection is shifting from AI **force multiplication** (assistants) to AI **force creation**: autonomous, intent-driven systems operating under delegated authority, scaling through compute rather than human labor.

It proposes a Trust Scope Framework and an Agent Control Plane blueprint to operationalize safe autonomy under adversarial conditions.
