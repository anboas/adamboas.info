What it does

From signal to source without changing systems.

The product compresses discovery, timing, money, and provenance into one review path while leaving the underlying evidence inspectable.

01

A now-first Gantt

Start in the current decision window, then move between three-year, seven-year, and all-time views without losing context.

02

Change intelligence

Surface new and updated procurement records, field-level changes, active solicitations, and time-sensitive schedule movement.

03

Evidence overlays

Layer reported schedules, solicitation windows, exact FPDS actions, follow-ons, funding, and bounded subaward evidence.

04

Money lineage

Move from budget request through account execution, awards, modifications, and source-linked public records.

05

Team calendar

Assign events to teams, layer workspace schedules, carry attendee context, and keep registration and review milestones visible.

06

Kiosk and wallboard

Project the shared calendar or tracked records as a fullscreen, distance-readable operating picture for conference rooms.

07

Shared operating context

Track records, assign events, save views, and preserve a workspace history across analysts, teams, and authorized agents.

08

Decision-ready exports

Export filtered evidence with the view URL, snapshot time, method, stable record identity, and source lineage attached.

Next product phase

Agentic discovery and augmentation will keep the operating picture moving.

Review-first by design

The next phase expands the existing verified augmentation pattern across industry events, contract records, and spending activity. New facts stay proposed until they are independently checked, source-bound, and safe to merge.

  1. 01 Discover

    Continuously find relevant industry days, conferences, solicitations, awards, and spending changes.

  2. 02 Augment

    Propose missing dates, official links, milestones, categories, money context, and record relationships.

  3. 03 Verify

    Independently test identity, evidence, confidence, conflicts, and the safety of each additive change.

  4. 04 Route

    Move approved intelligence into the Gantt, team calendar, wallboard, alerts, and source-linked review.
